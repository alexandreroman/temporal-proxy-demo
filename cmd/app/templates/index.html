Temporal Proxy Demo

The application dials one plaintext endpoint and knows nothing about what serves it. temporal-proxy owns the upstream, the TLS, the credentials, the Namespace rewrite and the payload encryption. Your keys stay where they already are — a cloud KMS, an on-prem HSM — and you can bring your own key service using its kms extension.
